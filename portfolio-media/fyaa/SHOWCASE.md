# FYAA portfolio showcase

Final file: `fyaa-portfolio-showcase.webm`

- Source: https://fyaa.io/ (the public developer portfolio)
- Duration: 28.500 seconds
- Resolution: 1440 × 900
- Encoding: WebM / VP9, 30 fps output, silent; original capture timing preserved
- Authentication: not required; fresh isolated browser context, no stored credentials

## Narrative

0–3.8s: animated purple hero, terminal-style introduction and 3D identity card.
3.8–8.9s: controlled scroll into the profile, with a pause on the engineering specialization and stack.
8.9–17.7s: continuous eased scroll through the professional journey into selected work.
17.7–21.6s: deliberate folder interaction and animated project-card expansion.
21.6–28.5s: open AARC's public case-study modal and hold on its product preview.

## Exploration and review

Explored the landing page, navigation, profile, experience, projects folder, AARC case study, skills, and contact destinations. Checked a 390 × 844 mobile viewport, mobile navigation and Arabic switching. No form was present on the portfolio page, and no transaction, message, upload, download of a CV, or production-data action was performed.

Reviewed all 543 captured source frames in sequential contact sheets, plus full-resolution opening, profile, folder and closing states. The encoded file was decoded without errors, and its dimensions and duration were checked with ffprobe. The final static pause is retained even though browser screencasting emits no further frames when the page stops changing.

No real credentials, tokens, private customer/employee records, personal contact details, private CV contents, browser chrome, DevTools or automation overlays appear. The owner's already-public name, portrait, employment history and company/project names remain. `fahad@portfolio.dev` and the authentication messages belong to the site's fictional terminal animation; no actual login was recorded. The small Controls label is the site's own keyboard-shortcut hint, not debug UI.

Excluded contact/CV content for privacy, external operational dashboards to avoid production data, and additional case studies, mobile/language switching and the long skills list to keep the film focused. The AARC view is a public portfolio case study, not an authenticated demonstration of its dashboards.

No website source or production data was changed. The video has not been integrated into the portfolio. Scripts, inspection images and review sheets are local supporting artifacts only. No authentication state was created.
